Effective: 21 October 2026 · com.aquadreamer.game

Privacy PolicyAccount & Data Deletion

This policy describes how TBB Studios handles information when you use AquaDreamer, including optional online features. For privacy questions contact tbb.studios.official@gmail.com.

Information processed

Game data: Fishing catches, fish collections, venues, achievements, in-game currency and preferences are stored on your device. Online play may also store game progress and activity on our backend.

Accounts: Optional email or Google sign-in may process an email address, user ID, provider details, username and friend code through our authentication provider.

Social and online gameplay: Friend requests, blocks, multiplayer trips, catches, leaderboard choices and optional shared statistics or racing-fish snapshots. Usernames and chosen gameplay information can be visible to friends or leaderboard participants; email addresses are not shown in public rankings.

Ads and technical data: The Android app uses Google AdMob and Google's User Messaging Platform. Subject to applicable consent and device settings, Google and advertising partners may process advertising identifiers, device information, approximate location inferred from technical signals, ad interaction and diagnostic data. Online service providers may receive IP addresses, request logs and device/browser information needed to provide and secure services.

Purposes

We process information to run and secure the game, authenticate accounts, store progress, provide multiplayer and friends features, display optional rankings, support players and deliver consent-managed advertising. TBB Studios does not sell personal information.

Services and sharing

Supabase provides our authentication and online database; Google provides optional Google Sign-In and AdMob/UMP advertising. Their services operate under their own privacy policies: Supabase, Google, and Google advertising. Other players see only information exposed by the game's multiplayer, friendship or opted-in ranking features.

Security and retention

Local saves stay on the device until removed or overwritten. Online data is retained as needed to provide accounts and services, or longer only where required for security or legal reasons. We use access controls and encrypted connections where supported; no service can promise absolute security.

Your choices and deletion

You may play without signing in, change available social or leaderboard sharing settings, manage available ad consent choices, or request access, correction or deletion of account data by emailing us. See the Account & Data Deletion page for steps. Deleting an online account does not automatically erase offline device saves.

Children

Use AquaDreamer according to its age classification on Google Play. Contact us if you believe a child has supplied personal information contrary to applicable requirements.

Changes and contact

We may revise this policy as the game changes and will update the effective date here. TBB Studios · tbb.studios.official@gmail.com.
