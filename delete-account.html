Effective: 21 October 2026 · com.aquadreamer.game

Privacy PolicyAccount & Data Deletion

To request permanent deletion of an AquaDreamer online account and associated personal information, use either option below.

Option 1 — In the game

Sign in, open Anglers' Club / online profile, select Delete Account and follow the confirmation steps. If this doesn't complete or you cannot access the game, use the email option below.

Option 2 — Email us

Send an email to tbb.studios.official@gmail.com with subject AquaDreamer Account Deletion Request. Include your in-game username and linked account email if known. Never send passwords, verification codes or identity documents. We may require proportionate verification to protect other accounts.

Email deletion request

Data deleted or retained

After verification, we remove the account and associated online profile, friendship, multiplayer and shared statistics under our control, except records we must retain for legal or necessary security reasons. Device-only saves are separate and must be removed using game/device data settings, if desired. You can also email us to ask about removal of specific personal data without closing the entire account.
